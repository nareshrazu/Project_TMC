Coriander is often harvested cut-and-come-again. The profile has no regrowth phase after harvest.

The physical system only has a pH-down pump. If pH drops too low or EC spikes too high, the system correctly stops dosing, but physical dilution requires manual human intervention (adding fresh water). UI/Buzzer alarms for out-of-bounds readings will be added in a future pass.

// Standardizes the EC reading to 25°C so dosing is always perfectly accurate
float compensatedEC = rawEC / (1.0 + 0.02 * (currentTemp - 25.0));
